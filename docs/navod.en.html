FIO-import — user guide

Turns bank statements and recurring payments into an XML file for Fio bank bulk payment import — without claiming the same cost twice.

1. What the app does

Every order goes from one account to one account (cost splitting); only the amount and the text differ. The app:

  1. reads card payments and ATM withdrawals from Fio and Revolut,
  2. adds recurring and mandatory payments (sewage, gas, electricity, phones…),
  3. compares them against the history of already claimed costs and flags what has been through before,
  4. gives you a review table — edit, enable or disable anything,
  5. generates the .xml that Fio accepts on the first attempt.

2. Step by step

Step 1 — due date

Fill in the due date at the top (one for the whole batch). It also drives the month written into recurring payment texts — e.g. plyn záloha Havlíčkova486-červenec 2026.

Step 2 — inputs

FieldWhere to get itRequired?
Fio — copy-pasteSelect the transaction rows in internet banking and paste them (Ctrl+V) into the field.optional
Revolut — CSVExport from the Revolut app. Upload the file with the button below the field.optional
History — CSV movements“Account movements” export of the receiving account. Incoming payments = what has already been claimed.recommended
Previous accepted XMLLast month’s file. Deposit amounts are carried over and history is extended.recommended

Recurring payments are not pasted anywhere — the app knows them from its template.

Quick period. Statement expenses can be limited to specific months with the Quick period control next to the date: the month number (0 = this, −1 = last, −2 = two ago — counted from the due date) × how many months back (1 = just it, 2 = plus previous). A preview shows the resulting period; Set fills it into “Transactions from–to”. Recurring payments are not affected.

Step 3 — Process

Click Process. With large statements it takes a moment — a blue bar runs across the top, the button switches to Processing… with a spinner, and the status line counts the seconds. When it finishes, a table of all rows appears, each with a status:

StatusMeaningDefault
NEWA new cost, not found anywhere else.goes into the XML
ALREADY_CLAIMEDSame date and amount are already in the Fio statement — really reimbursed.excluded
ALREADY_GENERATEDYou already generated it once (ledger / previous XML), but it is not in the Fio statement — maybe you did not upload the XML to the bank. It stays in the proposal; if you never sent it, switch it on.excluded (switch on manually)
DUPLICATE_IN_BATCHThe same cost appears twice in this batch (typically Fio and Revolut, or overlapping periods).excluded
An excluded row is never deleted. It stays visible, struck through, and you can switch it back on. Legitimate duplicates exist — two Lidl purchases on the same day for the same amount are not an error.
A recurring payment can be excluded too. A deposit (gas, phone, TV…) that already left this month and appears on the receiving account’s statement is recognised by its text and flagged ALREADY_CLAIMED — even when the bank replaces the ½/¾ share with a question mark. If you haven’t sent it yet, switch it back on with one click.

Step 4 — review and edit

  • “In XML” checkbox — toggles the row.
  • Amount and message text are editable in place (changed deposits, fixes).
  • Filters above the table — by source, status, or mandatory only.
  • Mandatory payments (sewage, gas, electricity) have a blue bar on the left; excluding one asks for confirmation.
  • A live total and order count run along the bottom.

Step 5 — generate and import

Generate XML downloads fio-import-YYYY-MM-DD.xml. Upload it in Fio internet banking under bulk import of domestic payment orders. Compare the checksum shown in the footer with what the bank reports.

3. Things to watch out for

Foreign currency. A Revolut row in EUR/PLN is excluded by default and carries a warning. The bank is paid in CZK — fill in the amount that actually left the account in CZK and enable the row.
Message length limit. Fio accepts roughly 140 characters. A longer text is rejected at generation time, naming the row to shorten.
History learns by itself. After each generated batch the app records the sent rows, so next time it recognises them as already claimed. That is why loading the receiving account’s CSV movements matters the first time.
Minimum amount (Settings). In the Settings tab you set the minimum expense amount (default 200 CZK, adjustable). Statement expenses below it are pre-set as off — small items not worth splitting. They stay visible and one click turns them on. Recurring and mandatory payments are not affected.
AI backend (Settings). In Settings you also pick which AI model is used for categorisation: Server default, Free — Workers AI, Paid — Claude, or Off. The choice is stored in the browser and takes effect on the next Process and in the AI indicator in the header.
Documentation — print and export. The Documentation tab summarises the app’s rules and the documentation layers. The Print, Export HTML and Export PDF buttons print it or save it as a self-contained file (with version, time and a link to the app).

4. AI categorisation

Revolut transactions carry no category or note. With Use AI categorisation ticked, the model fills in a category, a note and a cleaned-up merchant name, and suggests excluding things that are not split (Netflix, transfers to self).

It runs on Cloudflare Workers AI for free by default (nothing to set up, and the data stays on Cloudflare). If the paid Claude backend is configured and runs out of credit, the app falls back to the free one automatically. You switch which backend is used in the Settings tab.

It is an assistant, not an authority — you can overwrite everything. If the AI is unavailable, the app carries on and says so.

5. When something looks wrong

SymptomWhat to do
A statement row did not show up at allOnly card payments and ATM withdrawals are taken; incoming payments and internal transfers are ignored on purpose.
Everything is ALREADY_CLAIMEDThe history you loaded already contains this batch (e.g. last month’s XML covering the same period). Check the date range.
The bank rejected the importReport exactly what the bank said — the format rules are hard-verified, so a deviation is a bug on our side.
Wrong month in recurring paymentsThe month is derived from the due date. Fix the date and hit Process again.
